Data Visualization: The Ethics of Representation

A comparative analysis of global secondary school enrollment using the World Bank Gender Equality Indicators dataset.

White Hat: Global Convergence in Education

Design Rationale & Transparency

Dataset & Transformations: This visualization uses the World Bank Gender Equality Indicators dataset. To provide a comprehensive global view, the data was aggregated by year, calculating the mean of the "School enrollment, secondary, female (% gross)" and "male (% gross)" columns across all available countries, filtering out years with missing data.

Design Choices: The goal of this "white hat" visualization is to provide an objective, transparent look at the historical progression of gender equality in education.

Black Hat: THE GENDER ENROLLMENT CRISIS

Manipulative Techniques Used

Deceptive Filtering: This "black hat" visualization intentionally distorts the viewer’s perception of the exact same dataset to manufacture a false narrative of inequality and decline. Instead of showing the full historical context, the data is selected to only show a narrow 5-year window where minor fluctuations naturally occurred.
